USE IT WITH YOUR OWN DATA

One signal. Arrival times. Sequence numbers.

Your CSV needs the columns below. received_ms is the time each reading reached your collector, in milliseconds. A 32-bit sequence counter increments once per sample. Add a unique boot_id when the device restarts.

received_ms,sequence,value,boot_id
0,0,22.4,boot-1
100,1,22.6,boot-1
400,4,22.8,boot-1

Up to 20,000 readings. CSV limit: 2 MiB; saved-session limit: 8 MiB. Preserve arrival order. CSV and session files are processed on this page; no measurements are sent to a server.

What a gap establishes

A sequence gap means those positions were not observed in this recording. A late arrival can fill the gap. A long pause means no reading arrived during that interval. Neither observation, on its own, proves a network fault or broken sensor.

What this version does

CSV inspection, range isolation and export, repeatable replay, session export and a Python capture utility. Physical-device validation and live browser serial connections are separate future milestones.
